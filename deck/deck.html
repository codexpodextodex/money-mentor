07A bill, from a photo

Photograph the bill.Check. Confirm.

  • The model reads the payee, the account number, the reference, the amount and the due date.
  • The engine recalculates the check digits in the account number and the reference, and has the amount and the due date read twice. A misread is caught.
  • Tested on 1,000 bills: 984 right and ready, 16 held back for you to check, none offered with a wrong field.

On Saturday we described this to S-Pankki's team as an idea. It works in the demo now.

No typing an account number, no virtual barcode. In this demo nothing is paid. The test: 1,000 made-up bills, rendered in three layouts and three languages, 15 Nov 2026.
